New Freud

Fine-tuning using LoRA to reproduce Lucian Freud’s distinctive painting style.

A Portrait Experiment

Lucian Freud (1922–2011) was a British painter known for closely observed portraits. Lucian Freud’s style is characterized by raw, psychologically intense realism, thick and textured brushstrokes, muted earthy tones, and an unidealized portrayal of human flesh and vulnerability.

As an experimental project, I chose the references and used an AI assistant to train a LoRA, a small add-on for Stable Diffusion XL 1.0.
